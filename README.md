### Three movies 
1. Knives Out (2019)
> A modern whodunit mystery. When a wealthy crime novelist dies under mysterious circumstances at his estate, an eccentric southern detective named Benoit Blanc is anonymously hired to investigate. Everyone in the dysfunctional, greedy family has a motive, leading to a web of lies, twists, and dark humor.

2. Arrival (2016)
> A cerebral sci-fi drama. After twelve mysterious alien spacecraft touch down across the globe, a linguistics professor is recruited by the military to find a way to communicate with the extraterrestrial visitors. As she begins to decipher their complex language, she experiences profound flashbacks that hold the key to understanding the true purpose of their visit.

3. The Truman Show (1998)
> A satirical psychological comedy-drama. An insurance salesman discovers that his entire life is actually a massively popular, 24-hour reality television show. Every person in his life—including his wife and best friend—is a paid actor, and his idyllic hometown is a giant, controlled studio set from which he must try to escape
